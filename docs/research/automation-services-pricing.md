# Competitive Pricing: Small-Business Automation Services

Research for Parameter (parameter.digital). Accessed 2026-10-14. Prices are in USD unless noted.

**How to read this document**

- **Published** means the price appears on the provider's own page, an official directory, an official tool pricing page, or a marketplace page.
- **Secondary** means the figure comes from a blog, guide, or survey, not from the seller.
- **Inference** marks Parameter-specific reasoning. Inferences are not market facts.
- **Access note:** Upwork, Fiverr, and Make's pricing page blocked direct fetching (HTTP 403, or the pricing table did not render). Figures for those pages come from search-engine extracts of the official pages, captured on 2026-10-14, and are marked "via search extract". Check them in a browser before quoting them.

---

## Summary

- **Free discovery is the norm.** Most comparable providers offer a free first call or "audit": Attuned Ventures, GrowwStacks, Hackceleration, AutomateNexus, Withn, Snow Cap, and several Zapier and n8n directory listings. When discovery is paid, it is usually a small, creditable fee: $125 (FlowForge), $297 for a 75-minute session (Flow Digital), $500 for an assessment credited to the build (AutomateNexus), or from $500 and refundable (Automation Consulting Services).
- **Most single-workflow builds cost $250–$5,000.** Small studios and freelancers sit at the low end: Attuned's "Workflow" tier is $250–$1.5K, and the UK firm AI Automate Company charges £400. Upwork's n8n page estimates $500–$2,000 for a single workflow. Agencies with case studies sit at the high end: GrowwStacks quotes $3K–$5K for simple integrations, Hackceleration builds start at $2,000, AutomateNexus speed-to-lead starts at $7,500, and Automation Consulting Services charges $5K–$15K.
- **Roofing-specific comparables exist.** Withn publishes roofing tiers from **$2,000 setup + $300/mo** (lead capture and CRM sync) to $5,000 + $600/mo. AutomateNexus sells a "speed-to-lead system" from $7,500. These are the closest anchors for Parameter's first offer.
- **Small-business support retainers cluster around $200–$600/mo.** Published examples: AutomateNexus care plan at $350/mo, Attuned at $200–$600/mo, and Withn at $300–$600/mo. Retainers that include new builds cost much more: $2K/mo at Automation Consulting Services and from £2,500/mo at AI Automate Company.
- **Freelancer rates set a low floor.** Upwork's own pages estimate $40–$90/hr for Zapier and $40–$100/hr for n8n. Featured profiles often list $7–$55/hr, and Fiverr gigs start at $15–$135. Established experts publish $150/hr (Chris Wray) and $250/hr (XRAY, a Zapier directory listing). Parameter should compete on outcome and accountability, not on hourly price.
- **Tool costs are small next to service fees.** Entry-level paid tiers cost about $9–$30/mo: Zapier Pro is $19.99/mo billed annually or $29.99/mo billed monthly for 750 tasks, Make Core is about $9/mo billed annually for 10k credits, and n8n Starter is €20/mo billed annually. GoHighLevel, which many roofing marketers use, costs $97–$497/mo.
- **Recommendation (inference):** Keep discovery free. Price a fixed-scope pilot at **$2,500–$4,000** (anchor $3,500), with a design-partner rate of about **$2,000** for the first 1–2 customers. Offer support at **$350–$750/mo** in two tiers. The client pays tool subscriptions directly.

## Pricing models compared

| Model | Who uses it (examples) | Pros for a new provider | Cons for a new provider |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hourly / hour blocks** | XRAY ($250/hr blocks), Chris Wray (from $150/hr), Flow Digital (from $1,950 packages), Upwork freelancers | Low pricing risk; easy for unclear work | Invites comparison with $15–$50/hr marketplace sellers; customer carries overrun risk; doesn't fit "measurable value"; penalizes efficient reuse |
| **Fixed project (scoped)** | Attuned, GrowwStacks, Hackceleration, AutomateNexus, Automation Consulting Services, AI Automate Company | Small businesses can budget for it; matches "fixed-scope pilot"; rewards reusable components | Parameter carries overrun risk; needs tight scope and change control; early estimates will be poor |
| **Paid discovery / assessment** | FlowForge ($125), Flow Digital ($297), AutomateNexus ($500, credited), Automation Consulting Services (from $500, refundable) | Filters low-intent leads; pays for mapping time | Most competitors offer it free; adds friction for a provider with no reputation; credit or refund terms are now standard |
| **Setup fee + monthly** | Withn ($2,000–$5,000 + $300–$600/mo), AutomateNexus build + $350 care plan | Recurring revenue; aligns with monitoring and support; typical for home-services vendors | Customers may question paying monthly for "something already built" unless value is reported |
| **Retainer (hours or capacity)** | Automation Consulting Services ($2K–$10K/mo), AI Automate Company (from £2,500/mo), Contra guidance ($1K–$5K/mo) | Predictable revenue; good for clients with a backlog | Too large for a single-workflow small business; hard to sell before trust exists |
| **Productized subscription ("unlimited requests")** | Wishup ($1,999/mo staffing), MondayAgents ($2,499/mo, Gumroad listing) | Simple to buy; scales with reusable templates | Open-ended scope; fits poorly with "start with one real problem"; above most small businesses' budgets |
| **Value-based / outcome-based** | Mentioned by Contra guidance; no published small-business examples found | Fits "make value measurable"; strong story once baselines exist | Needs trustworthy baseline data and attribution; payment risk; complicated for a first deal. Better as a later experiment or a small bonus component |

**Inference for Parameter:** A fixed-price pilot plus an optional monthly support plan matches both the company's principles and the strongest small-business comparables (Withn, AutomateNexus, Attuned). Avoid selling hourly. If hourly is needed for out-of-scope work, publish a rate that sits clearly above marketplace rates.

## Recommended price structure for Parameter

All of this section is **inference** grounded in the evidence above. It is a starting hypothesis to test, not a validated price list.

### 1. Discovery session: free

- **Format:** 45–60 minute call, plus a short written summary within 2 business days. The summary covers a process map of the discussed workflow, 1–3 opportunities, what would be measured, and whether a pilot makes sense.
- **Example (roofing):** walk through the last 5–10 inquiries from first contact to inspection booked. Identify where leads wait, where data is re-entered, and what happens after hours.
- **Rationale:** Most comparables (Attuned, GrowwStacks, Hackceleration, AutomateNexus audit, Withn, Snow Cap's first consultation, and most Zapier and n8n directory listings) offer a free first step. A pre-revenue provider with no case studies should not add friction here. The company docs already plan a free conversation.
- **Guardrail:** If discovery grows beyond about 2–3 hours of mapping (multiple workflows, data audit), offer a **paid process assessment at $500, credited in full to the pilot** if signed within 30 days. This matches AutomateNexus ($500, credited) and Automation Consulting Services (from $500, refundable).

### 2. Fixed-scope pilot: $2,500–$4,000 (anchor $3,500)

- **Scope:** one workflow with up to ~3 connected systems. Includes:
  - a baseline measured before go-live
  - build and testing on real data
  - error alerts and run logs
  - a human-review step for exceptions
  - a short runbook and staff walkthrough
  - a 30-day measured run with a results review
  - a written exit plan: the client owns the accounts and automations, and there is a documented off-switch
- **Example (roofing):** web-form, phone, and email inquiries are captured into the CRM or a sheet, with an instant acknowledgment SMS or email, routing to the right person, an inspection-booking prompt, and follow-up reminders for leads that aren't booked. Measures: time to first response, % of leads acknowledged within X minutes, % booked to inspection, and staff minutes per lead.
- **Timeline:** about 2–3 weeks to build and launch, then a 30-day measurement period.
- **Payment:** 50% at signing and 50% at go-live (the same split as Automation Consulting Services' simple builds). Change requests are quoted separately in writing.
- **Design-partner rate:** about **$2,000** for the first 1–2 customers, in exchange for baseline data access, a results review, and permission for an anonymized case study. Don't go lower. It would undercut later pricing and put Parameter at Fiverr and Upwork price levels.
- **Rationale:**
  - **Roofing anchor:** Withn's equivalent roofing lead-capture tier is $2,000 setup + $300/mo.
  - **Small-workflow bands:** Attuned is $250–$1.5K for one workflow with 1–2 systems and $1.5K–$5K for 3–5 systems. Upwork's n8n estimate is $500–$2,000 for one workflow and $2,000–$5,000 for multi-app builds. Hackceleration starts at $2,000. GrowwStacks quotes $3K–$5K for simple 2–3-app work.
  - **Ceiling:** AutomateNexus speed-to-lead is from $7,500, and Automation Consulting Services is $5K–$15K. These are established firms. A new provider should stay below them.
  - **Effective rate:** $3,500 for roughly 25–35 hours of work is about $100–$140/hr. That is above marketplace rates ($40–$100/hr on Upwork) and below established experts ($150–$250/hr).
  - **Differentiation:** The measured baseline, the 30-day results review, and the exit plan justify a premium over marketplace builds. Few comparables publish these; AutomateNexus is closest.

### 3. Ongoing support: $350–$750/mo (two tiers)

| Tier | Price | Includes | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Care** | $350–$450/mo | Monitoring and failure alerts; fixes when connected tools change; up to ~2 hours of small adjustments; monthly one-page results report against the pilot measures; response within 1 business day | Keep the lead-intake workflow running and report monthly response-time and booking metrics |
| **Care + Improve** | $650–$750/mo | Everything in Care, plus ~5 hours/month of improvements or one small new workflow per quarter, and a quarterly review | Add estimate follow-up reminders after the intake workflow is stable |

- **Terms:** month to month with 30 days' notice (AutomateNexus uses the same terms). Tool subscriptions are billed to the client directly, not marked up.
- **Rationale:** Published small-business support plans cluster at $200–$600/mo: AutomateNexus $350, Attuned $200–$600, Withn $300–$600. Secondary guidance (lowcode.agency) gives $300–$600 for 1–5 Zaps. Retainers of $2K/mo and above (Automation Consulting Services, AI Automate Company, Contra guidance) cover new builds and capacity, which a one-workflow customer doesn't need yet. The monthly results report ties support to "make value measurable" and answers the "why pay monthly" objection.

### Out-of-scope work

Quote new workflows as fixed-price add-ons, using the pilot price as a guide; additional workflows for an existing client are often cheaper because of reuse. If an hourly rate is needed, use **$125–$150/hr**. That is in line with Chris Wray's $150 and below XRAY's $250. Avoid leading with it.

## Open questions / what to validate with prospects

1. **Willingness to pay for the pilot.** Present the $3,500 anchor and watch the reaction. Track whether objections are about price, about trust (no case studies), or about the timing of value. Test the $2,000 design-partner framing on early prospects.
2. **Value of a recovered lead.** Ask for average job value and close rate for repair and replacement jobs, and how many inquiries go unanswered or are followed up late. Use the answers to state pilot ROI in the customer's own numbers. No roofing job values were researched here; don't assume them.
3. **Setup + monthly vs. fixed pilot + optional support.** Roofing vendors (Withn) bundle a monthly fee. Test whether prospects prefer a lower setup fee with a required monthly plan, or a one-off pilot with optional support.
4. **Paid vs. free discovery.** Track how often free discovery leads to a pilot proposal. If many calls go nowhere, test the $500 credited assessment.
5. **Existing tools.** Which CRM or field software is in use (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, GoHighLevel, spreadsheets)? Tool choice affects build effort and the client's tool bill.
6. **Who buys and the budget threshold.** Is there a spending level (e.g. under $2,500) the owner approves without deliberation? Does the office manager influence the decision?
7. **Comparison set.** Ask what else they have tried or been pitched: marketing agencies, GoHighLevel resellers, freelancers, CRM add-ons. Their reference price may come from bundled marketing retainers rather than automation builds.
8. **Support expectations.** What response time do they expect when lead intake breaks? This decides whether $350–$450 Care is enough or a faster-response tier is needed.
9. **Data gaps in this research.** Upwork, Fiverr, and Make figures came from search extracts because direct fetches were blocked; re-check them in a browser. The Zapier and n8n directory budget filters didn't render values, and no published rates were found for Make partners.
